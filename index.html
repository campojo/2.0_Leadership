Before You Begin

Understand your natural leadership tendencies.

This leadership assessment is designed to help you better understand your natural leadership tendencies and preferences. Honest and thoughtful responses will provide the most accurate and useful results.

1Be honest

There are no right or wrong answers. The goal is to reflect your authentic self, not who you think you should be.

2Do not overthink, but do not rush

Read each question carefully and choose the response that feels most natural or describes how you typically behave.

3Use strong answers when they truly fit

Most situations are not black and white, but strong agreement or disagreement is appropriate when it genuinely applies.

4Find a quiet moment

Complete the assessment in a comfortable space with minimal distractions, ideally in one sitting.

5There is no perfect leader

Different leadership styles are effective in different situations. Understanding your style is a step toward adaptability and self-awareness.
